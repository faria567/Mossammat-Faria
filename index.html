    Frequently asked questions

    What's the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?
    SEO is about ranking in traditional search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is about being the answer AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude give when someone asks a question. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is about being cited inside Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. They overlap heavily — strong E-E-A-T, clear entity signals and well-structured content help across all three — but each has its own visibility surface to track.
    How long does it take to see results?
    Technical fixes and quick-win content can move the needle within a few weeks. Meaningful organic traffic growth typically shows up over 3–6 months, and AI-platform visibility (being cited in ChatGPT or AI Overviews) tends to follow once a site's topical authority is established. I report progress monthly rather than promising a fixed date.
    Do you work with small or local businesses?
    Yes — most of my recent client work has been with local service businesses (real estate, home services, driver training) alongside larger remote engagements. Local SEO and Google Business Profile optimization are part of my core service list for exactly this reason.
    What do you need from me to get started?
    Access to Google Search Console and Analytics (or an invite to set them up), a sense of your current priorities and target markets, and any existing brand or content guidelines. From there I usually start with a technical and content audit before proposing a plan.
    Do you write the content yourself?
    Yes. I write SEO-optimized blogs, service pages and landing pages myself, built around search intent, semantic SEO and topical authority rather than keyword-stuffed drafts.
    Can you work with my existing website platform?
    Most likely — I've worked directly in WordPress, Shopify, Wix and Squarespace, and I'm comfortable with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and Laravel for anything that needs custom changes.
